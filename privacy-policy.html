Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Image Generator ("Application", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application. By using the Application, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Image Generator is an AI-powered image creation and editing app. You can enter a text prompt, optionally attach reference photos from your camera or gallery, generate or edit images with artificial intelligence, save your generation history, and purchase credits for additional generations.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to operate the Application and provide its features.

a. Information You Provide

  • Account Information: You may use the Application as a guest with anonymous authentication, or sign in with email and password, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple (Apple sign-in is available on iOS). Depending on the method you choose, we may process your email address, display name, profile photo URL, and phone number (if provided by your sign-in provider). Guest accounts do not require an email address.
  • Profile Information: You may optionally set a display name and upload a profile photo, which are stored in your user account.
  • Prompts, Photos, and Generation Data: When you create or edit an image, we process the text prompt you enter, any reference photos you select (up to the limit shown in the Application), generated result images, and related metadata (such as task status, model identifiers, and timestamps). Source and result images are stored in Firebase Storage and associated records in Cloud Firestore.
  • Purchase Information: When you buy credit packages through in-app purchases, transaction processing is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive purchase confirmation data needed to credit your account; we do not receive or store your full payment card details.
  • Support and Account Requests: If you contact us by email (for example, to request account deletion), we receive the information you include in your message.

b. Information We Collect Automatically

  • Usage and Analytics Data: We collect usage data through Firebase Analytics, such as selected app events (for example, first onboarding open) and general technical usage signals that help us understand how the Application is used and improve performance and user experience.
  • Push Notification Token (FCM Token): If you allow push notifications, we collect and store a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token linked to your account so we can send service-related notifications.
  • Device and App Information: We may collect device type, operating system version, app version, and similar technical identifiers for authentication, delivery of notifications, analytics, and troubleshooting.
  • Local App Data: The Application may store preferences and cached media locally on your device (for example, via secure storage, SharedPreferences, or on-device cache) to improve loading speed and remember settings such as onboarding completion.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To authenticate you, process AI image generation and editing requests, deliver generated images, and maintain your generation history.
  • Credits and Purchases: To manage your credit balance, validate in-app purchases, and apply purchased credits to your account.
  • Personalization: To display your profile and saved generations within the Application.
  • Communication: To send push notifications (when enabled) and respond to support or account requests.
  • Analysis and Improvement: To analyze usage through Firebase Analytics, configure app behavior through Firebase Remote Config where applicable, and develop new features.
  • AI Processing: To send your prompt and any selected reference photos to our backend and AI image generation providers so we can create or edit images for you.
  • Security and Account Management: To protect the Application, manage accounts (including guest-to-registered account linking), and process deletion requests.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate the Application:

  • Google / Firebase: For anonymous and social authentication (Firebase Auth), database storage (Cloud Firestore), file storage (Firebase Storage), cloud functions (Firebase Cloud Functions), analytics (Firebase Analytics), push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), and remote configuration (Firebase Remote Config).
  • AI Image Providers: Your text prompts and any reference photos you submit may be processed by third-party AI image generation services (currently via Kie.ai / Seedream models) through our Firebase Cloud Functions, solely to generate or edit the images you requested and to create related task records.
  • Apple and Google: For Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, and in-app purchase processing, according to their respective terms and privacy policies.
  • Device Sharing: If you use the share feature, the generated image is shared through your device’s native share sheet with the apps or contacts you choose. We do not operate an in-app community feed.

4. Data Security and Retention

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS) and Firebase security rules. We retain your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Application, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

You can delete individual generation tasks from your history within the Application. You can delete your account from profile settings (registered accounts may need to re-authenticate). Account deletion removes your authentication account and associated data from our systems to the extent technically feasible, subject to reasonable backup retention periods. You may also contact us at the email below to request deletion.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information. Within the Application, you can:

  • View and Manage Content: Access your generation history and profile.
  • Update Profile: Change your display name and profile photo in profile settings (for registered accounts).
  • Delete Content: Remove saved generation tasks from your history.
  • Delete Account: Permanently delete your account through profile settings, or by emailing us. For guest accounts, include details that help us identify your account (such as your user ID if shown in the app).
  • Notification Preferences: Enable or disable push notifications in your device settings.
  • Analytics: You may limit certain analytics collection through your device privacy settings; some features may still require basic technical data to function.
  • Sign-In Choices: You may continue as a guest or link a permanent account using email, Google, or Apple (where available) at any time.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction, such as 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

7. International Users

Your information may be processed and stored on servers operated by our service providers, which may be located outside your country of residence. By using the Application, you consent to such processing where permitted by applicable law.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date above. Material changes may be communicated through the Application. Your continued use of the Application after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
